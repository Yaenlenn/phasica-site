Temperature Is Not a Straight Line

A brief note on temperature variation across the menstrual cycle.

Core body temperature fluctuates in response to subtle hormonal transitions. Throughout the menstrual cycle, resting temperature drops slightly during the follicular phase, rising marginally after ovulation as progesterone peaks.

These thermal shifts are small—often measuring mere fractions of a degree—yet when recorded continuously at rest, they form a clear cyclical pattern unique to each body.

Observing temperature trends over successive cycles allows Phasica to contextualize daily variations, aligning physiological measurements with personal baseline rhythms.
