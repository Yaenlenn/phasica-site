01 Introduction

Phasica treats physiological data as deeply private personal observations. This Privacy Policy outlines how your individual health measurements, including heart rate variability, nocturnal temperature, sleep signals, and menstrual cycle inputs, are processed within your personalized model.

02 Information We Collect

We process only physiological signals and user-entered context required to construct your personalized model. This includes HRV readings, resting heart rate, sleep duration metrics, temperature variations, and cycle symptom logs.

03 How Information Is Used

Your physiological measurements are interpreted strictly to generate individual predictions and detailed analysis of recovery, energy, and cycle phases. We do not monetize personal baseline data or share individual records with third parties.

04 Data Retention

Personalized model parameters and historical signal archives remain accessible to you for continuous trend analysis. You maintain complete control over data export and deletion at all times.

05 Your Choices

You may reset your personal baseline, delete specific signal history, or erase your entire Phasica account profile directly from within the Phasica application settings.
